Guide to reading the entries

  • In-game description: The official text shown within the game.
  • Real effect: The quantified effect and the technical explanation derived from the game engine.
  • Activation condition: The exact conditions under which the ability activates on the field.
  • Areas of the field: The 18-meter lines are measured from the halfway line across the entire width (not from the penalty area lines); the zones are mirrored when the teams switch ends.

Reliability note: The numbers come directly from the guide (obtained via reverse engineering of the game engine). Data regarding animation-related ability stats (e.g., a 0.20s latency) and "unreleased" abilities are not confirmed.

Shooting Skills

Heading

Standard
Improves the accuracy of headers as well as the frequency of downward headers.
Real EffectInjects +5 to Heading accuracy and forces ballistic trajectories downward toward the turf, reducing goalkeeper save windows.
Activation ConditionStandard header attempts

Activation ConditionCurled or finesse shot from outside the penalty box

Real EffectOptimizes initial ball lift trajectory and shortens chip shot wind-up latency, improving lob accuracy over the keeper by +12%.
Activation ConditionChip shot attempt (L1 + Shot)

Standard

Real EffectSuppresses gyroscopic spin and injects random sinusoidal oscillations along Y/Z axes, producing erratic knuckleball movement that confounds keepers.
Activation ConditionPower strike with full instep and power > 65%
