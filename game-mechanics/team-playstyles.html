Section 1.8

Team Playstyles

Each Play Style dictates how your AI-controlled players move during three key phases of a match.

Match Phases

Attacking Phase

When you have possession of the ball.

Defending Phase

When the opponent has the ball.

Transition Phase

Right after you lose or regain possession.

Play Style Details

Possession Game

Best ForPatient build-up play, short passing, and controlling tempo.
AttackingTeammates move closer to the ball carrier for safe, short options; rare aggressive runs into open space.
DefendingMaintains a high defensive line to squeeze opposition in their half with high pressure.
TransitionKeeps compact shape, offering nearby passing options to prevent re-tackles.

Quick Counter

Best ForFast, aggressive attacks and high-pressing defense.
AttackingFrontlines immediately burst into space for rapid scoring opportunities, prioritizing speed.
DefendingAggressive high defensive line; players press high up the pitch to force errors near opponent's box.
TransitionForward runners sprint into space immediately before opponent reorganizes.

Long Ball Counter

Best ForSolid defense, low-block stability, and direct counter-attacks.
AttackingAttackers spread out and make runs behind defense while midfield/defense sends direct long balls forward.
DefendingDrops into a deep, compact low block near own box to absorb pressure.
TransitionAttackers instantly sprint into open space left behind by attacking opponent.

Out Wide

Best ForWingers, wide midfielders, and crossing into the box for tall strikers.
AttackingOverloads flanks to stretch defense; central players/midfielders crash the box for crosses.
DefendingForms a solid mid-block in center pitch to funnel play or regain ball in midfield.
TransitionTeammates push outward toward sidelines immediately to stretch pitch.

Long Ball

Best ForUsing a tall, strong Target Man to hold up play and lay off to midfielders.
AttackingDefenders hit direct long balls toward a designated Target Man; midfielders collect second balls.
DefendingDefensive line sits deep to form a protective block and soak up pressure.
TransitionDefenders fall back; strikers hold ground as targets while wide midfielders tuck inside centrally.
