Individual Instructions

Control specific player movements and behaviors during a match, overriding standard AI habits and playing styles for custom tactical setups.

Tactical Breakdown

1. Defensive

Core Behaviour: Tells the player to stay back during attacks instead of pushing forward into the opponent's half.

Field Analysis: The player strictly holds the defensive line. They won't make overlapping runs, keeping your defense solid and preventing easy counter-attacks.

Best Use Case: Apply to fullbacks (RB/LB) or a DMF. It gives you a reliable backline that never gets caught out of position.

2. Anchoring

Core Behaviour: Stops the player from drifting sideways across the pitch when the team shifts left or right.

Field Analysis: The player stays locked in their assigned channel. They can still run forward into the box, but they won't wander off to the opposite side of the field.

Best Use Case: Perfect for a strong, physical Center Forward to ensure you always have a central target for passing and hold-up play.

3. Counter Target

Core Behaviour: Tells an attacker to stay high up the pitch and ignore all defensive work when the opponent has the ball.

Field Analysis: The player stands idle on defense, saving their stamina. As soon as you win the ball back, they are immediately free and ready to receive a counter-attack pass.

Best Use Case: Ideal for Second Strikers (SS) or Strikers (CF) to quickly catch opponents on the break.

4. Tight Marking

Core Behaviour: Assigns your defense to stick closely to a dangerous opponent using a zonal system.

Field Analysis: Whichever defender is closest to the marked opponent will press them tightly. If the opponent moves across the pitch, your defenders pass off the marking duty smoothly without breaking your formation shape.

Best Use Case: The safest and most effective way to shut down key playmakers without creating huge gaps in your defense.

5. Man Marking

Core Behaviour: Forces one specific defender to follow an opponent everywhere on the pitch.

Field Analysis: The defender shadows the target everywhere. However, smart opponents can pull your defender out of position, opening up dangerous holes in your backline.

Best Use Case: High risk. Use with caution—Tight Marking is almost always a safer choice.
