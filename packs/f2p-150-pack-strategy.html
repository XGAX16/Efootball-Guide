F2P 150-Pack Strategy

When should you spend your coins? Managing your eFootball coins effectively is crucial for Free-to-Play (F2P) players. This guide outlines the exact probabilities and thresholds to consider before pulling from a 150-player Epic pack.

Spending Thresholds & Odds

< 3,000 Coins
< 30 draws
Don't Spend
  • ~49% chance of getting at least 1 Epic.
  • ~51% chance of walking away with nothing.
3,000 – 6,000 Coins
30–60 draws
Spend only if pack is exceptional
With 60 draws:
  • ~79% chance of getting at least 1 Epic
  • ~31% chance of getting at least 2 Epics
  • ~6% chance of getting all 3 Epics
6,000 – 10,000 Coins
60–100 draws
Spend if 2+ Epics upgrade your squad
With 100 draws:
  • ~97% chance of getting at least 1 Epic
  • ~74% chance of getting at least 2 Epics
  • ~29% chance of getting all 3 Epics
13,500 Coins
150 draws
Guaranteed Pool Clear
  • 100% guaranteed chance to clear the entire pool and get all 3 Epics.
Tip: Consider stopping early if you obtain 1 or 2 target Epics.

The Golden Rule for F2P Players

The most common mistake is spending 900–1,800 coins on every new pack. This often leaves you with no Epics—and not enough coins when a truly great pack arrives.

Best practice: Save your coins, skip average packs, and only invest when a pack contains at least 2–3 Epics that would genuinely improve your squad. In the long run, this is the most efficient strategy.
Note: When there is a 10 spin discounted to 500 it is always worth buying it.
