Skill Transfer Guide

When you transfer extra skills to a player, transferring a skill from a free player (e.g., Lombardi) to the exact same player ID is significantly cheaper. By stacking all 5 desired skills onto a single Lombardi card first, you can save a substantial amount of GP.

Step-by-Step Guide

1

Roll the Skills Individually

  • Sign 5 separate copies of Lombardi.
  • Use one Skill Training Program on each copy until you get the 5 specific skills you want.
Result: You now have 5 different Lombardis, each carrying 1 distinct skill (you can keep them aside to build an archive of skills).
2

Stack the Skills onto One Card

  • Take Lombardi #2 and transfer him to Lombardi #1.
  • Take Lombardi #3 and transfer him to Lombardi #1.
  • Repeat this for Lombardi #4 and Lombardi #5.
Result: You now have a single Lombardi (#1) holding all 5 accumulated skills.
3

The Final Transfer

  • Perform one final transfer from Lombardi #1 (now carrying all 5 skills) directly to your target main squad player.
Result: Your main player receives all 5 skills at once.

Cost Breakdown

Method Process Total Cost
1-by-1 Method Expensive Transferring 5 times individually from a Lombardi to your target player (~257k GP per transfer). 1,285,000 GP
Stacking Method Cheap Stacking all 5 skills onto one Lombardi card first, then transferring to your target player once. ~879,000 GP
Total Savings
~400,000 GP (~30% less)
