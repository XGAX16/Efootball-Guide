How It Works

Weeks = (target balance − current balance) ÷ weekly income, rounded up
  • All regions: 50 coins per week from the daily login + 100 coins per week from PVP Event Matches = 150 coins per week.
  • Regions with ADV: an additional 70 coins per week from ADV rewards = 220 coins per week.
