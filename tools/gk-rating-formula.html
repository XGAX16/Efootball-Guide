Formula

GK Score = (Sum of Core 3 Stats − 250) + ((Upper Body Eq. Height − 180) × 1.5) + Playstyle Score + Form Score
  • Playstyle Score: Offensive GK = 5, Defensive GK = 0.
  • Form Score: Fixed B (Epic / Big Time) = 3, Live = 0.
  • Neuer: gets an extra point for his unique player ID.
