How It Works

P(X = k) = C(K, k) × C(N − K, n − k) / C(N, n)
P(X ≥ m) = sum of P(X = k) for every k from m up to the maximum possible
  • Hypergeometric distribution: the chance of getting k special cards in n pulls from N cards containing K special ones, without replacement.
  • Impossible results: if the pool has fewer special cards than requested (K < m) or you pull fewer cards than requested (n < m), the result is 0%.
