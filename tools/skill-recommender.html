How It Works

  • Source: the ratings in the Skill Suggestions page. Skills with no rating for the chosen position are not considered.
  • Ranking: MUST first, then YES, then CAN. Skills your player already has are left out.
  • Ties: skills with the same rating are ordered by how important their skill class is for that position (the sum of its ratings), then by their order in the guide.
