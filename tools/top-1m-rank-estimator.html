How It Works

Estimated final position = current position + (average hourly increase × remaining hours)
  • Linear model: historical data shows the decay is best approximated as a steady increase in position per hour.
  • Scenarios: the five scenarios differ only in the hourly increase used, from the least to the most pessimistic.
  • Colors: green = final position between 1 and 1,000,000 (inside the Top 1M); red = above 1,000,000 (outside).
